A few ways we can help

Free help

Your listing is always free. We're also glad to help with a flyer, a game sheet or getting the word out, at no charge.

Paid advertising

If you want more, we can put together advertising for your hall, paid from your bingo account like any other advertising expense.

Featured bingo event, $100

Running a special bingo night or fundraiser? We feature it, labeled, for people searching near your hall on Find Bingo, until the day of the event. Paid like any other advertising expense.

A website for your hall

We can build and keep up a simple website for your hall, for a fee, if you would rather pay for a service than partner.

Ask about a website

Community partnership

A separate thing from advertising. Some organizations choose to name Veteran Home Guardians as one of their recipients. If you do, we ask for a modest share, usually 5 to 10 percent of what you give, not all of it. At minimum, it should cover visits to veterans in nursing homes in your county. Anything beyond that helps us reach counties that have no local support. It is never required, and it never affects your listing or our help.

How partnership works

We can come out and see your hall if you'd like. We can just as easily talk by phone.

How we keep it simple and above board

  • Advice is free. We don't charge for advice about running your game, and we don't work your sessions or handle your money (ORC 2915.09).
  • Paid advertising is advertising, invoiced and paid by check or electronic transfer from your bingo account, as Ohio requires (ORC 2915.10).
  • Partnership and advertising are kept separate. One never depends on the other.

If you are unsure whether something fits your organization, ask the Charitable Law Section at 800-282-0515 or your attorney. This page is not legal advice.
