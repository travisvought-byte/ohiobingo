Looking for a cause? Consider veterans in nursing homes.

Veteran Home Guardians is a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 33-4637180). We pay veterans to visit other veterans living in long-term care, and we publish a free resource guide for veterans and families.

Ohio lets a licensed organization give the net profit from traditional bingo to charitable purposes listed in its license application (ORC 2915.09). Instant bingo has its own distribution rules (ORC 2915.101). If your organization would like to name us, the Charitable Law Section at 800-282-0515 can tell you how to add a recipient.

  • We are not asking for all of it. We ask for a modest share, usually 5 to 10 percent of what you give. At minimum, it should cover visits to veterans in nursing homes in your county. Anything beyond that helps us reach counties that have no local support.
  • We will publish every gift we receive from a bingo organization and what it paid for.
  • Your listing and our promotion work never depend on it. Listings are free to every hall.
