Find veterans and local providers who do paid work near you. Each listing shows the service, the area covered, and the provider's own hourly rate.

Listings

The directory is opening now. We are collecting the first listings. If you do paid work for veterans and families, add yours below.

Service types we list

  • Home repair and handyman
  • Ramps and accessibility
  • Lawn, snow, and property care
  • Cleaning and hauling
  • Rides and errands
  • Companion visits
  • Moving help
  • Paperwork and benefits help

How it works

  • Providers submit their own listing and set their own rate.
  • You contact the provider directly and agree on the work between you.
  • Veteran Home Guardians does not employ, supervise, or guarantee providers. Rates and details are self-reported. Confirm insurance, licensing, and references yourself before hiring.
